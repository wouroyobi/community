Development

Development in Wouro Yobi is community-led, rooted in Mbororo heritage, and focused on education, sustainable livelihoods, youth leadership and social cohesion.

Explore the Development Story

Development Philosophy

Wouro Yobi's development approach builds on local knowledge, traditional leadership, community participation and partnerships. The aim is to strengthen opportunities while preserving the identity and heritage of the community.

Mbororo Community

Wouro Yobi is part of the wider Mbororo (Fulani) cultural landscape in Cameroon. The community's history includes pastoral traditions alongside agriculture, education and community-led development.
