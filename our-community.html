Where is Wouro Yobi?

Geographic Identity

Wouro Yobi is located in the Mayo-Darlé Municipality of the Adamawa Region of Cameroon.

  • Coordinates: 6°30'21.2"N, 11°30'08.1"E
  • Decimal: 6.505889, 11.502250
  • Setting: Rural agricultural landscape
  • Character: Farms, forests, community settlements and traditional leadership.

This location is more than a point on a map—it is the foundation of the community's identity and development story.
